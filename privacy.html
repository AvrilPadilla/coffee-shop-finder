Privacy at crunch.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

At crunch., we want students to understand what information we collect and how we use it. This page explains how your information is handled when you use our website.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your school email address and information needed to manage your account.

If you save places, we store the locations you've bookmarked and associate them with your account.

We may also store your selected college and website preferences in your browser.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to create and manage your account, confirm your school email, provide saved places, and support the features of crunch.

3. Student verification

A verified student account means that the user has confirmed access to an email address from a supported college domain.

Email verification does not independently prove current enrollment or student status.

4. How information is stored

crunch. uses Supabase to provide account authentication and store saved places. Supabase processes information needed to provide these services.

We use account-based access controls to restrict access to saved places. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices

You can explore basic features of crunch. without creating an account. Saving places requires a verified account.

You can remove individual saved places through the Saved Places page.

To request removal of your account and associated personal information, contact us using the address below.

6. Younger users

Some college students may be under 18. Additional privacy requirements may apply depending on a user's age and location. Our policies for younger users will be reviewed before public launch.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as crunch. develops new features. Updates will be reflected on this page.
